## Implemented

- Shared medical-sharing feature: owner inbox/summary, filtered cursor pages, fresh request details/history, capability-scoped approve/reject/revoke, required idempotency keys, and private state invalidation.
- Treating-doctor records appear only inside an actual InProgress ticket encounter: authorized overview/timeline, hidden placeholders and protected-token requests, sent status/details, clinical encounter/item reads and independently authenticated private attachments.
- Own encounter visibility/history use the published canChangeVisibility and rowVersion from the authoritative encounter response; no policy or medical rights are enabled by frontend permission alone. Medical-sharing DTOs and cursor/query/body contracts were refreshed from live OpenAPI.
- Doctor practice readiness reads authorized configuration and the actual public practice projection. Activation, online booking and effective bookability are distinct; configuration/schedule/pricing repair links reuse existing scoped sections.
- Exact backend-origin/path verification now controls JWT/locale injection and 401 handling. Printable tickets use authored DOM/textContent, no dynamic raw HTML, no inline scripts, and no whole-queue fallback when the popup is blocked.
- Walk-In registration reuses the reception matching/guardian workflow. A practice/token-bound memory handoff returns the confirmed patient to fresh walk-in options without an automatic ticket mutation.
- Latest-request handling covers doctor search/location references, family pages/history, dependent transactions/receipts and ticket inspection. Follow-Up history starts independently of bookable-patient metadata. Medical timeline refresh preserves its filter/cursor domain.
- Already-implemented Doctor My Patients/diagnostics/historical reads, practice commercial entitlements, historical-document upload and parent child-intake integrations were audited and closed as frontend wiring. Separate QA and policy decisions remain open.
