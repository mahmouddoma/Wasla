# Wasla

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Users

Patients and authorized guardians book and manage appointments. Reception staff operate within currently assigned practices and delegated permissions. Doctors manage their own practices and reservations. Administrative users review registrations and inspect operational reservations.

## Product Purpose

Provide bilingual healthcare appointment and practice operations. The user confirmed implementation of all outstanding requirements in the WAS Jira implementation audit.

## Capabilities and Constraints

Angular 21, standalone components, typed API contracts and feature-owned workflows. Existing domains expose public business APIs. Jira is the source of endpoint and business requirements. Server capabilities, availability, metadata and permissions are authoritative. Reservation inspection remains operational; administrative views omit booking notes and clinical records.

## Brand Commitments

Preserve the existing Wasla enterprise dashboard design, Bootstrap-first layout, neutral subtle borders, restrained surfaces, consistent tokens and typography. Details and reviews use slide-over drawers. Every mutation provides ToastService feedback.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Complete Arabic and English translations, RTL/LTR logical layout, keyboard focus and labeled controls. Responsive desktop, tablet and mobile layouts.

## Evidence on Hand

AGENTS.md, existing Angular components and design tokens, connected WAS Jira tickets, and docs/jira-implementation-audit.md. Live backend verification depends on backend availability; no deployment success is implied.
